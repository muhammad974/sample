FAQ

Questions, Answered

Everything you need to know before starting a project with us.

How long does website development take?

Most websites launch in 2–4 weeks depending on scope and revisions.

Is the domain included in the website price?

No — domain and hosting are billed separately. We're happy to guide you through purchasing and setup.

Is advertising budget included?

No — our fee covers campaign setup and management. Your ad spend on Meta or TikTok is paid directly to the platform and is separate.

Do you provide social media content?

Yes — our Social Media Management service includes content creation, professional captions, hashtags and daily posting, handled by our team.

Can you create custom AI automation?

Yes — AI Automation is scoped and priced around your specific workflow, so we start with a short discovery call.

Do you manage Shopify stores?

Yes — our Shopify Store Management service covers ongoing setup, upkeep and support for your store.

How do I start a project?

Book a call or message us on WhatsApp — we'll discuss your goals and recommend the right services to get started.
